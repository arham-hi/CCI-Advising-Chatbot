Students in the Bioinformatics (BA) concentration are introduced to the collection, informatics analysis, and interpretation of data derived from genomic and biological macromolecular investigations. The major focus is on DNA, the molecule that provides the building plan for all living things. Students will receive training in up-to-date methods for data handling and interpretation while developing an understanding of critical issues in bioinformatics research design, statistical data analysis, and the application of genomics domain knowledge.
